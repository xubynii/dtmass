# Downtown Massing Tool — a first study in eight steps

Open the live tool: https://xubynii.github.io/dtmass/ (Chrome or Edge, laptop screen). In the tool, press **Help → Start the interactive tour**: a card walks you through these steps on the real model, waits until you have done each one (or does it for you), and moves on. The tour also starts by itself on a first visit.

1. **Pick your lot.** On **Site**, press *Change site* and click a downtown parcel. Under *Restrictions*, confirm the two items marked "needs verification" (ODP density area and height area); until you do, the checks that depend on them stay at "needs verification".
2. **Set the target.** On **Design**, open *Development target*: type the floor area you need and the tower floorplate you prefer, and the number of parking levels. The massing regenerates as you type.
3. **Set the program mix.** Drag the sliders until the bar reads 100%, or press *Normalize*. Each use gets its own floors in the stack.
4. **Choose a typology.** Pick Slender Tower, Podium + Tower, Split Towers on a Shared Podium or Courtyard Podium + Tower. The panel shows what you asked for against what fits on the lot, and names any conflict (height, density, floor plate) with the change that would resolve it.
5. **Shape it by hand.** In the 3D view, switch between **Face** and **Mass** above the view. Face: drag any face to push or pull it, drag the roof to add storeys, click a face to type an exact size. Mass: drag a block to move it on its floor. Blocks stop at the setback limit and at each other. The two cores follow the masses automatically.
6. **Read the checks.** On **Check**, every rule shows its current value, the requirement, the clause (a link to the source) and a next step. *Show in model* points at the block or storey concerned.
7. **Save and compare.** Press *Save version*, change something, save again. **Compare** puts two versions side by side with matched drawings, program shares and what improved or got worse.
8. **Make the report.** On **Report**, the sheets build themselves: project data sheet, synopsis, findings, capacity, constraints, recommended moves, fire separations, cores and the appendix of every check. Print to PDF from the browser.

Useful keys: Ctrl+Z undo, Esc cancels a drag, F toggles Face / Mass, Delete removes the selected block.

Remember: this is a screening tool. A pass here is not a permit; the README lists every source with its date and what the tool does not check.
