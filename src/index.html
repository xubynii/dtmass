Working in the studio

Pick a lot in Site, shape the massing in Design, review Check, save versions and Compare them, and export a Report. Tabs keep your view and selection.

About the checks

Screening checks of a massing model against the Downtown ODP, VBBL 2025 Part 3 and Parking By-law 6059. They do not establish compliance. Surrounding buildings © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); site data from the City of Vancouver Open Data Portal.
